# LUMEN Germany launch recommendation

LUMEN should enter Germany with a €2.19 base price and a clean-label, everyday functional positioning aimed first at Urban Wellness Professionals, with Fitness & Gym-Goers as a secondary audience. The initial distribution test should prioritize DTC Online and Gym & Office, where the balanced price produces contribution of €1.16 and €1.13 per unit respectively, while Retail/Grocery should be introduced selectively for reach and trial.

The geographic hypothesis is Berlin first and Munich second, followed by expansion only after repeat purchase and channel economics are observed. Use May for setup and sampling, then run the main demand test in June–July, when the supplied seasonality index is strongest.

This plan deliberately does not optimize for maximum short-term reach or maximum unit margin: it sacrifices the mass-market volume of €1.79 and the margin of €2.59 to preserve a more credible balance between premium perception, adoption, and learning speed. All German demand, city, and timing conclusions remain hypotheses until the pilot generates real German evidence.

## Next operating decision

Set a pilot budget and success thresholds in the decision cockpit. Track trial, repeat purchase, contribution after acquisition spend, and channel-level learning separately; use those observed results to decide whether to scale Retail/Grocery or revise price and positioning.
