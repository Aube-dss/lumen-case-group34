Decision rules

Scale: repeat purchase, contribution per unit and city-level economics meet plan for two consecutive months.

Pause / reset: discounting is needed to clear inventory, blended contribution weakens, or retained LTV:CAC remains below 3:1 after optimisation.

This cockpit is an aid to judgment, not a German sales forecast. It keeps the assumptions visible because Germany has no LUMEN sales history yet.
